# Rivu — Reviews & UGC

Marketing copy for the App Store listing, launch posts and outreach.

Every claim below is implemented and verified. Nothing here is aspirational —
if a feature moves, update this file, because these lines end up on the
listing and in ads.

---

## One-liner

**Collect more product reviews — with QR codes, automatic emails and AI that
helps your customers actually write them.**

## Short description (App Store, ~100 words)

Rivu turns buyers into reviewers. Print one QR code on your packaging and
customers can review straight from their phone — no login, no per-product
codes. Post-purchase emails go out automatically. And when a shopper opens the
review form, AI suggests what they might say, tailored to that exact product,
so they write a real review in seconds instead of closing the tab.

Your reviews then show up as star ratings in Google search results, and in a
widget you can shape to match your theme — eight summary layouts, full control
of colour and type, or your own HTML if you want it exactly right.

---

## The problem it solves

Most stores collect almost no reviews. Not because customers are unhappy —
because writing a review is work. A blank text box after a purchase is a
request for unpaid effort, and most people close the tab.

Rivu attacks that from three sides: make it effortless to *start* (QR on the
box), automatic to *ask* (post-purchase email), and easy to *finish* (AI
suggests the words).

## What Rivu does

### Collecting reviews

- **One QR code for your whole store.** Print it on packing slips, thank-you
  cards or receipts. A customer scans, enters the email they ordered with, and
  Rivu looks up what they actually bought. No separate code per product.
- **Per-product QR codes** for places where the product is already known — a
  checkout thank-you page, for instance.
- **Automatic post-purchase emails.** Set a delay in days and Rivu emails
  every buyer, once, per product. Anyone who already reviewed that product is
  skipped, and unsubscribes are honoured.
- **On your product pages.** A "Write a review" form built into the widget,
  with photo and video uploads.
- **Bring your existing reviews.** Import a CSV export from Judge.me, Loox,
  Stamped or Yotpo — columns are detected automatically, no renaming.

### AI review suggestions

The differentiator, and the reason stores using Rivu collect more.

- Around **120 AI-written suggestions per star rating**, written for the
  specific product being reviewed — not generic filler.
- **Each suggestion is offered to one shopper only.** Once someone uses a
  line, nobody in your store is ever shown it again. No two of your reviews
  read the same, which is the tell that makes a review section look fake.
- **10 languages**, and you can let shoppers choose their own.
- Falls back to hand-written templates if the AI is unavailable, so the form
  is never empty.

### Showing them off

- **8 summary layouts** — Modern Card, Minimal, Compact, Left Sidebar,
  Stacked, Horizontal Bar, Icon + Percentage, Split Panel.
- **4 review layouts** — list, grid, masonry, carousel.
- **4 review-form styles** — Basic, Card, Minimal, Dark.
- **Full control of colour, font, size, spacing and borders**, so it matches
  your theme rather than approximately matching it.
- **Rating badge** for near your product title and on collection cards. Click
  it and the page scrolls to the reviews.
- **Build your own layout (Pro).** Write your own HTML and drop in
  `{{stars}}`, `{{average}}`, `{{count}}`, `{{breakdown}}`,
  `{{write_button}}` and `{{review_list}}` wherever you want them.

### Getting found

- **Google star ratings.** Rivu emits schema.org `Product` and
  `AggregateRating` markup so your ratings can appear under your products in
  search results. This is the single highest-value thing a reviews app does
  for traffic, and it's on by default.

### Turning reviews into marketing

- **Shareable review graphics.** Turn any review into a social-media image in
  8 styles — Minimal, Bold, Pastel card, Gradient pop, Polaroid, Receipt,
  Bordered classic, Big quote — with your logo watermarked on.
- **Review reward discount codes.** Automatically issue a real, single-use
  Shopify discount code to anyone who leaves a review. Percentage or fixed
  amount.

### Staying in control

- **Publish instantly or moderate first** — one toggle. Pending reviews are
  counted where you'll see them.
- **Approve, unpublish or delete** any review.
- **Export everything to CSV** whenever you want. Your reviews are yours.

## Angles for posts

Pick one per post — don't list features.

**The QR angle.** "Your packaging is the only marketing channel every single
customer definitely sees. Put a review QR on it." Good with a photo of a
printed code on a box.

**The AI angle.** "People don't skip reviews because they're unhappy. They
skip because writing one is work." Then show the suggestions appearing.

**The Google angle.** "Those little gold stars under search results aren't
automatic. This is how you get them." Screenshot a rich-snippet result.

**The fake-review angle.** Strong and rarely used by competitors: "Most review
apps hand every customer the same suggested text. Rivu retires a suggestion
the moment it's used, so no two of your reviews read the same."

**The price angle.** Judge.me's paid tier is $15, Loox starts near $13. Lead
with the QR flow and AI rather than price — cheapest is a weak position for
something a merchant trusts with their storefront.

## Words to use, and to avoid

Use: *collect, effortless, in seconds, matches your theme, your reviews are
yours.*

Avoid: *revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class, 10x.* And never quote a
conversion-lift percentage you haven't measured on real stores — one
screenshot of real reviews on a real product page beats an invented statistic,
and it can't be challenged.
